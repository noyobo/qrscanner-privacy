隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 14 日

相机与照片

相机画面在设备本地处理，用于识别二维码和条形码。通过系统照片选择器选择图片时，仅使用所选图片进行识别。本应用不会上传相机画面或所选照片。你可以在系统设置中撤销相机权限；相册识别仍可使用。

历史记录与设置

扫码内容、收藏和偏好设置保存在本地。你可以删除单条记录，或在首页清空历史记录。多码选择时的画面快照仅保存在内存，不写入相册。系统设备备份可能包含本地应用数据，具体取决于系统设置。

剪贴板、分享与链接

复制操作会将扫码内容写入系统剪贴板，开启自动复制后也会自动写入。本应用不读取剪贴板。分享或打开扫码结果时，内容交由你选择的系统功能或其他应用处理，这些服务适用各自的隐私政策。

数据收集

当前版本没有广告、统计分析、用户账号或开发者运营的服务器，不向开发者发送扫码内容，不跟踪用户。

联系我们：noyobo@gmail.com

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Camera and photos

Camera frames are processed on your device to recognize codes. When you select a photo using the system picker, only that selected image is used for recognition. The app does not upload camera frames or selected photos. You can revoke camera access in iOS Settings; photo recognition remains available.

History and settings

Scanned content, favorites and preferences are stored locally. You can delete individual records or clear all history on the home screen. Camera snapshots are kept in memory for code selection and are not added to your photo library. Device backups may include local app data according to your system settings.

Clipboard, sharing and links

Copying writes scanned content to the system clipboard, including automatically if you enable Auto Copy. The app does not read the clipboard. Sharing or opening a result sends that content to the system or app you choose; those services have their own privacy policies.

Data collection

This version has no advertising, analytics, accounts or developer-operated server. It does not send scanned content to the developer and does not track users.
